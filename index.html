What’s In Season

December 19 – 30, 2026

🍲

HK Hot Pot & Roast Goose Peak

December = peak hot pot season in HK. Yat Lok’s 3-Michelin roast goose is at its richest. Sham Shui Po dai pai dong wok hei air feels electric at 16°C.

🍲

Seoul Samgyetang Season

Ginseng chicken soup is winter soul food. Hot broth, cold air, heated palace floors, possible snow. Korean BBQ tastes 2x better at -5°C.

🍲

Taipei Hot Pot & Beef Noodle

December average 18°C — perfect night market weather. Mango is out of season but coffin bread, oyster omelets, peppered pork buns are at peak.

✨

Macao Christmas Lights

Senado Square wrapped in Portuguese-meets-Asian Christmas lights. Light Up Macao festival runs into early January. Pastel pink Senate House in full glow.

♨

Korean Bathhouse Weather

Jjimjilbang culture peaks at -5°C outside. Jade rooms, salt saunas, sleeping halls, sauna eggs + sikhye. Christmas Eve at a jjimjilbang — very Korean.

🎄

Banqiao Christmasland

Asia’s biggest Christmas light festival runs through Dec 28. 30M LEDs, projection mapping on City Hall. Arriving in Taipei on Christmas Day — lands directly into this.

Rosewood Hong Kong

Tsim Sha Tsui waterfront. 322 rooms, but every room is harbour-facing. Asaya spa, Tasting Library, the legendary Asaya Kitchen, and the Manor Club lounge. #1 on World’s 50 Best Hotels list 2024.

“The Victoria Harbour view never gets old. Falling asleep with the skyline on full neon.”

Star Ferry — HK’s $0.50 Icon

The 1888-founded Star Ferry from Tsim Sha Tsui to Central. 8-minute crossing. Lower deck. National Geographic listed it as one of the “50 places of a lifetime.” The fare is HK$5 (~$0.65 USD).

Culture

Dim Sum at Lin Heung Kui (Trolley Style)

The last great traditional trolley dim sum in HK. Aunties push steam carts through the room. You stand up, lift the lid, point. Order har gow, siu mai, char siu bao, cheung fun, mango pudding. Loud, chaotic, perfect.

★ NYC dim sum is order-from-menu. Trolley dim sum is a dying art — experience it before it’s gone.

M+ Museum — Asia’s Tate Modern

Herzog & de Meuron 2021 building on the West Kowloon waterfront. 33-story LED facade. Greatest collection of post-war Asian visual culture on earth. Tsang Tsou-choi calligraphy, Pipilotti Rist video installations, Wong Kar-wai film archive.

Culture

Mak’s Noodle (Central) — Wonton Soup Origin

Hwaseong Fortress — UNESCO World Heritage

Suwon is the literal origin of Korean galbi (marinated short rib). Yeonpo Galbi is the most famous galbi restaurant in Korea, opened 1945. Family-style. Charcoal grill at your table. Order Suwon wang galbi — king-cut. Lettuce wrap, raw garlic, ssamjang.

Food
★ This is where Korean BBQ began. Tasting the original is impossible anywhere outside Suwon.

Korean Folk Village

1 NIGHT

Morpheus by Zaha Hadid

Zaha Hadid’s final completed building (2018). The world’s first free-form high-rise exoskeleton. 770 rooms in a curved black-mesh tower. 3-Michelin-star Alain Ducasse at Morpheus on-site. Skin-tearing piece of architecture.

“Sleeping inside Zaha Hadid’s final building. There is no equivalent anywhere.”

Hotel shuttle to Senado Square. 25 buildings + plazas dating 1557–1990s. Senado Square (pastel wave-tile Portuguese plaza), St. Dominic’s Church (1587, butter-yellow), Largo do Sé, Ruins of St. Paul’s (1602 cathedral facade, only the front wall remains, stair-rising into the sky).

A-Ma Temple — The City’s Origin

1488 Taoist temple to the goddess Mazu. Macao is literally named after her (“A-Ma Gao” = Bay of A-Ma). Stone gateways, ancient banyans, six worship halls climbing the cliff. The smoke of incense fills your hair.

Culture
6:30 PM

Lord Stow’s Bakery (Coloane Village) — Original Macao Egg Tart

The famous pastel de nata of Asia. Lord Stow’s 1989 original location in Coloane Village. Crisp lard-puff pastry, caramelized custard top. Eat 3-4. Bring 6 back. The Coloane Village around it is the old fishing-village heart of Macao — cobblestones, yellow chapel.

Food
★ The original Macanese egg tart at its 1989 source
7:30 PM

Dinner: Fernando’s on Hac Sa Beach

Legendary Portuguese restaurant on Macao’s black sand beach. No menu, no website, no reservations, no air-con. Roast suckling pig, African chicken (Macao’s signature: piri-piri + coconut + Goa fusion), clams in white wine, charcoal-grilled sardines, sangria poured from a jug. Anthony Bourdain dedicated an episode to it.

FoodOnly here
★ Portuguese-Macanese-African fusion at the literal end of a black sand beach in Asia. No NYC analog.
10:30 PM

The 8 at Grand Lisboa — 3-Michelin-Star Late Tea

The only 3-Michelin-star Chinese restaurant in Macao. Stay in for a late dessert + drinks course. Bird’s nest with bird’s nest soup, roast pigeon. The dining room is one of the most beautifully designed rooms in Asia.

Food
